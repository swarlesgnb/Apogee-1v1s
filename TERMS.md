# Terms of use

Apogee is a free ranked ladder for KovaaK's, run by one person. These terms say what you
can expect from it and what it expects from you.

**This is a draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** It describes how the
service is intended to work. If you need it to carry legal weight in your jurisdiction,
have it looked at first.

## What Apogee is

Apogee reads the stat files KovaaK's writes after every run, uploads what it parses, and
settles a ladder from the result. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with
KovaaK's, The Meta, Valve, or Steam. It is not a game and it does not modify one.

Using it requires a Steam account, a copy of KovaaK's, and pointing the client at your
stats folder. Nothing is ever typed in; every score comes from a file the game wrote.

## What you agree to

**Play your own matches.** Scores must come from you, playing, on your own account. Do
not submit runs played by anyone else, runs produced by any tool that plays for you, or
files you have edited.

**One account.** One Apogee account per person. Alternate accounts made to sit lower on
the ladder, to farm a rating, or to evade a suspension are not allowed.

**Do not attack the service.** No attempts to forge submissions, exhaust the API, extract
other people's data, or interfere with anyone else's matches.

**Be civil.** Your display name comes from Steam and is shown to opponents and on
leaderboards. Names chosen to harass, impersonate, or abuse get changed or removed.

`FAIR-PLAY.md` describes exactly what is checked, what is not, and why. Read it: it is
specific about the limits of what verification can catch.

## What happens if you break them

Runs can be voided. Matches can be voided. Ratings can be reset. Accounts can be
suspended or removed. Where a decision rests on a judgement call rather than an automatic
check, you can ask for it to be looked at again, and it will be.

Verification tiers are not accusations. A run marked *suspect* still counts; it is held
for review, and the reason is shown on screen rather than hidden.

## What Apogee owes you

**Your data, described plainly.** `PRIVACY.md` lists every field that leaves your
machine. If the code and that document ever disagree, the code is the bug.

**Rules in the open.** Seasons, ranks, and thresholds are published in
`data/seasons/`, and a published season is frozen. Nothing about your standing is
adjusted quietly.

**Your account, on request.** Ask and your account and its runs will be deleted.

## What it does not promise

This is free software run at one person's expense. There is no uptime guarantee, no
support commitment, and no promise that any season, rating, rank, or leaderboard position
survives a reset, a migration, or a bug. Ratings are a number in a database, not property.

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law
allows, the author is not liable for any loss arising from using it.

Ranked play needs an opponent pool, a server, and an account with a history behind it.
Any of those can be unavailable.

is public. Continuing to use Apogee after a change means accepting it.
