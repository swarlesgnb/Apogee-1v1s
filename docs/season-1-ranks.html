Apogee · Season 1 · draft

Twenty-five ladders, 146 ranks

Apogee is ranked 1v1 for KovaaK's. You queue a category, play three scenarios, and the ladder moves. Dynamic Clicking, Evasive Switching, Precise Tracking, Reactive Tracking, Speed Switching and Static Clicking each rank separately, so being near the top of one says nothing about the others, and an overall rank is derived from all of them.

Each category is cut into four bands: Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert. One set of scenarios cannot measure both a first-week player and a good one, so each band is a whole benchmark with its own scenarios, its own ranks and its own top. A player holds a rank in every band they have played, which makes a category four short ladders rather than one long one, and means nothing ever has to ask whether the top of a band sits below the bottom of the next.

Below are the names and the colours, and nothing else. Nothing is published yet, so all of it is still cheap to change.

What I am asking

  1. Do the names read as a progression? Is it obvious which end is good?
  2. Does each ladder's theme land, or is it trying too hard?
  3. Would you be pleased to be told you are any of these?
  4. Each band names its own ranks, so a category's twenty-three names are four separate sets rather than one climb. Does that hold, or do the bands want separate themes?
  5. Which colour is the first one you would change, and what to?

How to read a rung

The colour exactly as the season stores it, filled, so a near-black is still visible as a chip. This is the only place on the page it is not adjusted.

The name on the client's own ground, which is where it lives.
Peregrine
The same name printed. A screenshot, a profile or a web page is a light ground, and a colour that survives only one of the two is not finished.
both names
Moved towards the opposite ground where the colour alone would be unreadable, by exactly as much as it takes and no more. That is what the client does, and what any surface showing a rank ought to. So a name that looks fine here can still be one the findings below name: the chip beside it is the honest one, and the ratios on the right are measured before the move, not after.

  • Fifty-seven colours sit on an edge of the RGB cubeFifty-seven of the 110 named colours have two channels pinned at 00 or ff. That is where a colour has no room left to be moved for either ground: as bright as it can get on paper, as dark as it can get on the client, and nowhere to go when one of the two needs it to give. Thirty-one of them already fail one of the two contrast checks above, which is what this is really a count of.

Season 1 is a draft. Names, colours, thresholds and even the number of ranks are all still free to change. Publishing freezes the season, because a rank cannot be re-cut under people who already earned it, so this is the moment to be picky.
