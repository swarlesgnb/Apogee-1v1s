Apogee · Season 1 · draft

Thirteen ladders, fifty-six ranks

Apogee is ranked 1v1 for KovaaK's. You queue a category, play three scenarios, and the ladder moves. Clicking, Tracking and Switching each rank separately — being near the top of one says nothing about the others — and an overall rank is derived from all three.

Each category is cut into four bands — Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert — because one set of scenarios cannot measure both a first-week player and a good one. Each band is a whole benchmark: its own scenarios, its own four ranks, its own top. A player holds a rank in every band they have played, so these are four short ladders per category rather than one long one, and nothing asks whether the top of a band is below the bottom of the next.

These are the names and colours. Nothing is published yet, so all of it is still cheap to change.

What I'm asking

  1. Do the names read as a progression — is it obvious which end is good?
  2. Does each ladder's theme land, or is it trying too hard?
  3. Would you be pleased to be told you're any of these?
  4. Each band names its own four ranks, so a category's sixteen names are now four sets of four. Does that hold up, or do the bands want their own themes?

Each named rank is shown three ways: a colour chip, the name on dark, and the name on light. If it disappears in one of those, that's worth knowing.

A rank gets shown outside the client too - a screenshot, a profile, a web page - and a badge that only survives one background is not finished.
Two ladders top out in the same colourPhoton Lance and Hare and HAL 9000 are both #ffffff; Ferret and Homosapien are both #ff0000. The top rank is the one people screenshot, so it wants to be the most distinctive colour on the page rather than a shared one.
One colour used on more than one rank#00fbff on Quasar (Overall) and Roadrunner (Switching · Advanced) and Robosapien (Tracking · Expert). Two ranks the same colour is two ranks that look like one.
Season 1 is a draft: nothing is published, so names, colours, thresholds and even the number of ranks are all still free to change. Once a season is published it is frozen — a rank cannot be re-cut under people who already earned it — so this is the moment to be picky.
