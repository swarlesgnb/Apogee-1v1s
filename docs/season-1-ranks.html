Apogee · Season 1 · draft

Four ladders, thirty-two ranks

Apogee is ranked 1v1 for KovaaK's. You queue a category, play three scenarios, and the ladder moves. Clicking, Tracking and Switching each rank separately — being near the top of one says nothing about the others — and an overall rank is derived from all three.

These are the names and colours. Nothing is published yet, so all of it is still cheap to change.

What I'm asking

  1. Do the names read as a progression — is it obvious which end is good?
  2. Does each ladder's theme land, or is it trying too hard?
  3. Would you be pleased to be told you're any of these?

Each rank is shown three ways: a colour chip, the name on dark, and the name on light. If it disappears in one of those, that's worth knowing.

Things I already know are wrong

Better to name them than have three people each spend their first message on the same ones.

Four colours vanish on one background Neolithic and Jackrabbit are pure white, so they disappear on anything light. Astrologist, Sloth and Galapagos Turtle are near-black and disappear on anything dark. A rank badge has to survive both.
Tracking's order isn't the actual sequence Homo erectus → Neanderthal → Homo sapiens → Caveman → Neolithic → Human. Caveman and Human sit after Homo sapiens, and Neolithic is an era rather than a species. Anyone who knows the order will notice, and the joke lands better if the ladder is right.
Two ladders both top out in the same red Supernova and AimGPT are both #ff0000. The top rank is the one people screenshot, so it should be the most distinctive colour on the page, not a shared one.
Clicking is untouched Still placeholders, and the ladder most in need of a theme. Switching went slow-animal to fast-animal and reads instantly — Clicking wants something with the same shape.
Season 1 is a draft: nothing is published, so names, colours, thresholds and even the number of ranks are all still free to change. Once a season is published it is frozen — a rank cannot be re-cut under people who already earned it — so this is the moment to be picky.
