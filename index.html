for KovaaK's.

Three scenarios, one opponent near your rating. Each round goes to whoever beats their own baseline by more. Scores come straight from KovaaK's stats files, so nothing is ever typed in.

Find your rank

Where do your scores land?

Every rank is a score on a scenario you can play today. Bring your runs and the table below fills itself in.

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\FPSAimTrainer\FPSAimTrainer\stats

See where they land

Read in this tab and never uploaded. In Chrome and Edge the page keeps watching, so a run you finish in KovaaK's shows up here within seconds.

How a match works

Your aim, against someone at your level.

The queue opens once 50 of your runs have uploaded, which the app does from the history KovaaK's already keeps. If you have played KovaaK's for a while, you are probably there already.

  1. 01

    Category

    Pick one of the six below, or all of them.

  2. 02

    Match

    You're paired with someone near your rating.

  3. 03

    Play

    Three scenarios in KovaaK's, as normal. Apogee writes the playlist for you.

  4. 04

    Results

    Each round goes to whoever beat their own baseline by more. Ratings settle on the server.

Season 1

The ranks.

Each category is graded in four difficulty bands, and each band is its own ladder. A rank on a scenario is the furthest column your best score reaches.

Provisional thresholds. Every threshold this season was predicted from the scenario file, because the scenarios are new and there are not enough real scores to cut ranks from yet. They get recut from real runs, and ranks can move when they do.
