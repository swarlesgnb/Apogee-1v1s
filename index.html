Ranked 1v1 for KovaaK's

You and an opponent near your rating play the same three scenarios. Each one goes to whoever beats their own usual score on it by the bigger margin, so it's about who plays above their level on the day. Scores are read from KovaaK's stats files, so nobody types anything in.

Below are all 164 Season 1 scenarios and the score each rank needs on them. Bring your own scores and see where you land.

Download for Windows Free, and the source is on GitHub.

Check your scores

Your best on each season scenario gets filled into the tables below.

C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\FPSAimTrainer\FPSAimTrainer\stats

Go to the tables

The folder is read in this tab and never uploaded. Chrome and Edge keep watching it, so a run shows up here a few seconds after you finish it.

How a match works

  1. Install the app and sign in through Steam. It uploads the runs already in your stats folder, and you can queue once 50 of them are in.
  2. Queue one of the six categories, or all of them. You get matched with someone near your rating.
  3. The app puts the three scenarios in a KovaaK's playlist. Play them like you normally would.
  4. Each scenario is scored against your own baseline on it: the median of your last 50 runs, or 90% of your best if that's higher. The bigger improvement takes the scenario. Ratings are worked out on the server, not in the app.

Season 1 ranks

Each category is split into four difficulty bands (Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert), and each band has its own ladder. Your rank on a scenario is the highest column your best score reaches.

All of these thresholds are provisional. The scenarios are new, so there weren't enough real scores to set ranks from. Each one was predicted from the scenario file and then adjusted using playtest runs and the same players' scores on KovaaK's leaderboards. They'll be recut from real runs once there are enough, and ranks can move when that happens.
