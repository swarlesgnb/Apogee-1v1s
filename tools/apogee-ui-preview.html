      Overall tier

      Your standing across all six categories. Tiers are shares of the population, so one keeps its meaning as the ladder grows.

      Category ladders

      four bands each, six ranks a band

      Four bands per category, each a whole benchmark with its own ranks. They overlap by two, so the next band's first ranks are reachable from this one. The overall tier is derived from all six categories.

      The pool

      One difficulty at a time. Your best on each scenario, and what the next rank asks for. A highlighted row is one your next rank is scored on.

      Nothing here was invented. Every scenario is drawn from a benchmark the community already grades itself against, so the marks under a name are the other ladders that run is worth something on — filled in once your score holds a rank there. Hover one for the rank and what the next asks.
